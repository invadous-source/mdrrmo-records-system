TechLink / About

FOUR MINDS.
ONE PLATFORM.

TechLink is a shared professional portfolio created for Wilan, Arman, Sherlyn, and Madelain. The idea is simple: keep the identity of each person while giving the group one polished digital home.

01 / The idea

People first.

TechLink is not a marketplace or a list of services. It is a portfolio-first platform where each professional can present their own skills, technology, experience, and projects.

02 / The structure

One shared identity.

The platform connects four individual profiles through a common visual language and navigation, making it easy for visitors to move from the collective to a specific professional.

03 / The future

Built to grow.

Authentication, dashboards, database-backed profiles, and private one-to-one conversations can be added later without changing the core public portfolio.

04 / The goal

Show the work.

As real biographies, technologies, and projects are added, TechLink becomes a stronger representation of the people behind the platform.
