A simple overview of what TechLink is today and what the platform is planned to become.

What is TechLink?

TechLink is a shared professional portfolio for four IT professionals: Wilan, Arman, Sherlyn, and Madelain.

Is TechLink a service marketplace?

No. The current direction is portfolio-first. The site is focused on people, technical skills, projects, and professional identity rather than public bidding or marketplace listings.

Can I view each professional separately?

Yes. Each professional has an individual profile page with space for their biography, skills, technologies, specialization, and work samples.

Can I contact a professional?

The public portfolio is being built first. A future phase will introduce login and private one-to-one conversations with a selected professional.

Will professionals see each other's private chats?

The planned messaging design is private: each professional will only see conversations directed to them. This will be implemented when the backend and authentication phases begin.

Will TechLink have accounts?

Yes, accounts are planned for a later phase. The current site is intentionally focused on the public portfolio experience.

Can the profiles be customized?

Absolutely. The placeholder content is designed to be replaced with the real biographies, skills, technologies, specialization, photographs, and project information of the four professionals.
